# RC3.4 Dynamic Marketplace Map Engine

- All eligible open jobs remain visible together.
- Camera automatically fits live marketplace entities.
- Accepted/cancelled jobs disappear through existing realtime subscription and trigger a refit.
- Selected mission highlights without replacing market state.
- Fastest guard route overlays the market while preserving the complete viewport.
- Agency range control: 5, 10, 15, 25, or 50 miles.
- Range is saved locally immediately and persisted to the authenticated agency through a constrained RPC.
- Jobs outside range are counted instead of silently disappearing.
