# RC3.2 Acceptance Gate

1. Open Agency Marketplace with at least one available Guard publishing GPS.
2. Click an open property card.
3. Confirm the property image, property name, verified address, mission type, and priority are displayed.
4. Confirm the nearest eligible Guard is recommended with profile image and current resolved street address.
5. Confirm the real driving route appears and road distance and ETA update.
6. Confirm returning to all markers removes the route projection.
7. Claim the mission and confirm the certified mission lifecycle remains intact.
8. Confirm only the real Notification Engine bell remains; mock header bell and message controls are absent.
