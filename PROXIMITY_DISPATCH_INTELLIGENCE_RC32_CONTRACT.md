# RC3.2 Proximity & Dispatch Intelligence Contract

The engine ranks only Agency Guards who are available and have verified coordinates. Selecting a marketplace property creates one shared route projection from the recommended Guard to the property through the Unified Map Engine.

Property presentation is authoritative: property name, client-uploaded image, verified address, mission type, priority, and closest-Guard recommendation.

Guard presentation is authoritative: profile image, name, live GPS freshness, reverse-geocoded current address, road distance, and drive ETA.

Before claim, the recommendation is advisory. After claim, Operations remains responsible for assignment and Mission Engine remains responsible for lifecycle state.
