# Mobile Privacy & Permission Disclosures

These strings are release-source copy. Native Info.plist/Android resources must use the applicable text when the native projects are created.

## Guard location — while using app
CO PILOT GUARD uses your location to show mission position, route progress, arrival status, and location attached to mission evidence.

## Guard location — background
CO PILOT GUARD uses your location during an active security mission even when the app is in the background so mission progress and operational safety status can continue updating.

## Guard camera
CO PILOT GUARD uses the camera when you choose to capture required mission evidence.

## Guard notifications
Allow notifications for assignments, agency messages, mission alerts, reminders, and active-mission updates. Android may also require notification permission to display the persistent notice while background mission location is active.

## Client notifications
Allow notifications for guard assignment, mission progress, messages, service updates, and other requested-security activity.

## Data-handling release rule
Do not claim that data is never collected, never retained, never shared, or never linked to identity unless the production backend/payment/provider behavior has been separately audited and supports that exact store disclosure.
