# CO PILOT Mobile Store Metadata

## CO PILOT SECURITY — Client
**Bundle ID:** `com.copilotsecurity.client`

**Subtitle:** Security when and where you need it

**Short description:** Request professional security, follow your assigned guard live, receive mission updates, and manage service from one secure app.

**Full description:** CO PILOT SECURITY connects approved clients with professional security service through the Co Pilot Security Marketplace. Request service for a property, follow an assigned guard during an active mission, receive status notifications, review mission activity and evidence, and manage billing from one secure workspace.

**Privacy summary:** The Client app uses account information, property/service details, payment-related information handled by the configured payment provider, device notification tokens, and location-related mission information needed to provide requested security service. Camera access is not required for normal Client operation unless a future feature explicitly requests it.

## CO PILOT GUARD — Guard
**Bundle ID:** `com.copilotsecurity.guard`

**Subtitle:** Your live security mission workspace

**Short description:** Receive assignments, navigate active missions, share live duty location, capture secured evidence, message your agency, and complete mission checkpoints.

**Full description:** CO PILOT GUARD is the field workspace for approved guards operating through the Co Pilot Security Marketplace. Guards can receive mission assignments, share location while an active mission requires tracking, capture mission evidence with the device camera, communicate during assignments, progress through checkpoints, and receive operational notifications.

**Location disclosure:** During an active security mission, CO PILOT GUARD may collect and transmit the guard's location, including while the app is in the background, so the assigned agency/client experience can display mission progress and support operational safety. Background tracking is tied to active mission operation and is not a general-purpose location history feature.

**Camera disclosure:** Camera access is used when the guard chooses to capture required mission evidence. Captured evidence is uploaded through the existing secured mission-evidence workflow.

**Notifications disclosure:** Notifications are used for assignments, agency/mission messages, operational alerts, reminders, and mission updates.
