# Mobile Store Asset Checklist

## Client — CO PILOT SECURITY
- Final app icon approved and exported for App Store and Play requirements.
- Launch/splash identity approved.
- Phone screenshots: sign in, request security, active tracking, mission activity, billing/settings.
- Store copy reviewed against STORE_METADATA.md.

## Guard — CO PILOT GUARD
- Final app icon approved and visually distinct from Client.
- Launch/splash identity approved.
- Phone screenshots: sign in, assignment, active mission/map, evidence capture, messages/settings.
- Background-location disclosure visible where store review requires it.

## Release rule
Assets are not marked complete by source generation. Final screenshots must come from the signed release candidate or a store-acceptable build representing it.
