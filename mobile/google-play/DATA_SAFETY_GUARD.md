# Guard — Data Safety Worksheet

This is a source-derived worksheet, not a final legal declaration. Verify production SDK/provider behavior before entering Play Console answers.

Potentially collected/processed data:
- Account identity and guard profile information
- Precise location during mission operations, including background location when active mission tracking is enabled
- Photos / mission evidence selected or captured by the guard
- Messages and operational mission activity
- Device push-notification token
- Assignment, checkpoint, arrival, completion and related mission status data

Purposes may include app functionality, account management, service fulfillment, operational safety, communications, fraud/security, and mission documentation.

Sensitive-permission disclosures:
- Precise/background location: active mission progress and operational safety
- Camera: guard-initiated mission evidence capture
- Notifications: assignments, messages, alerts, reminders and mission updates; Android persistent notification may accompany background mission location

Questions requiring production verification:
- Exact retention periods
- Exact third-party sharing/service providers
- Encryption-in-transit declaration
- User deletion mechanism and records retained for legal/security/business obligations
