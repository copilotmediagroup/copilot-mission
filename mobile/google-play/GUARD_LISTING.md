## Full description
CO PILOT GUARD is the field workspace for approved security professionals operating through the Co Pilot Security Marketplace.

Receive mission assignments, navigate active work, share duty location when mission tracking is active, capture required mission evidence, communicate with your agency, receive operational notifications, and complete mission checkpoints from one mobile workspace.

Background location may be used during an active security mission so authorized mission participants can receive continuing progress and operational-safety updates when the app is not in the foreground. Background tracking is tied to active mission operation rather than general-purpose location history.

Camera access is used when a guard chooses to capture mission evidence. Notifications support assignments, agency messages, mission alerts, reminders, and operational updates.
