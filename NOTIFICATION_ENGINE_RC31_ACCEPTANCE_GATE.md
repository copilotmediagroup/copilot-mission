# RC3.1 Acceptance Gate

1. Open Agency portal in Live Test using an approved Agency account.
2. Keep the browser open on any Agency page.
3. Create a standard Client request in another session.
4. Confirm a realtime toast appears without refresh and unread badge increments.
5. Dismiss the toast; open the bell and confirm the notification persists.
6. Click the notification and confirm Agency navigates to Marketplace.
7. Repeat for Priority and Emergency; confirm distinct treatment.
8. Claim the job from one Agency and confirm it disappears/withdraws for competing Agencies.
9. Refresh and confirm read/unread state persists without duplicate toast replay.
